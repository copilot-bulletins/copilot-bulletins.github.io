Biweekly #9 · July 15 to July 31, 2026 · Decision-Makers Edition

🎯 This edition's thread

The second half of July centered on measurement and cost control. As Copilot spreading across teams becomes usage-based, GitHub shipped the instrumentation leaders need: an impact dashboard, repository-level metrics, and credit governance tied to cost centers. Alongside a widening frontier-model catalog, the signal for decision-makers is clear — Copilot investment is now quantifiable, governable, and tied to measurable business outcomes.

⚠️ Heads up

  • Gemini 2.5 Pro and Gemini 3 Flash deprecated — Deadline: July 31, 2026 — These models are retired across all Copilot experiences. Ensure teams migrate to supported models and confirm alternatives are enabled in your model policies.

🚀 Take action

  • Tell your Copilot impact story with the new usage metrics and reporting capabilities.
  • Govern AI spend by managing credit pools and budgets in cost and spending management.
  • Review which frontier models fit your teams and set policy in AI model configuration.
  • Questions or need support? Reach out to your GitHub team, we're here to help.

🏆 Top 5 updates

1. Prove Copilot's impact with a new dashboard

A new metrics impact dashboard for enterprise administrators and organization owners goes beyond who is using Copilot to show the effect on delivery. For decision-makers, it turns adoption into a defensible impact narrative for stakeholders and budget conversations.

→ Changelog: New Copilot usage metrics impact dashboard

2. Govern AI spend per cost center

AI credit pools can now be managed directly in the billing UI when creating and editing cost centers. This gives finance and engineering leaders a straightforward lever to allocate, cap, and track Copilot spend across business units without API workarounds.

→ Changelog: AI credit pools for cost centers in the billing UI

3. Repository-level metrics reach general availability

The Copilot usage metrics REST API now reports per-repository, daily activity for coding agent and code review. Leaders gain granular visibility into where AI delivers value, enabling data-driven decisions on where to expand or focus enablement efforts.

→ Changelog: Repository-level GitHub Copilot usage metrics generally available

4. A widening frontier-model catalog

Copilot added Claude Opus 5, Grok 4.5, and Gemini 3.6 Flash this period, spanning deep reasoning to fast agentic coding. Broad model choice reduces vendor lock-in risk and lets organizations match capability and cost to each workload.

Sources:
→ Changelog: Claude Opus 5 is now available in GitHub Copilot
→ Changelog: Grok 4.5 is now available in GitHub Copilot
→ Changelog: Gemini 3.6 Flash is now available in GitHub Copilot

5. Faster access to new models by default

A new global default enablement policy turns on generally available models for Copilot Business and Enterprise automatically, instead of requiring manual activation. Teams get innovation sooner while administrators retain policy control to restrict models when needed.

→ Changelog: Default model enablement for Copilot Business and Enterprise
